## Simple for teams. Rigorous by design.

<div align="center">

**Keep your tools. Keep your people in control. Make the evidence visible.**

</div>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><h3>Connect</h3><p>Bridge the systems where requirements, code and evidence already live.</p></td>
<td width="33%" valign="top"><h3>Control</h3><p>Verify revision states and prerequisites for human reviews and approvals.</p></td>
<td width="34%" valign="top"><h3>Demonstrate</h3><p>Report gaps, preserve traceable references and support reproducible verification.</p></td>
</tr>
</table>

**Human decisions remain human.** AI may prepare analysis, but it cannot grant QARA approval. MCP checks can detect gaps; reliable access enforcement, independent audit records, retention and approvals need appropriate system and procedural controls.
